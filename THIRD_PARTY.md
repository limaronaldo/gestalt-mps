Code used for the IBQ codec is covered by the upstream [Apache 2.0 license](https://github.com/TencentARC/SEED-Voken/blob/c1544efff959ba18d453089071307a7115698551/LICENSE). Cite Sun et al., [IBQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02692).
- [Gestalt checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/GeWu-Lab/Gestalt/tree/42a8b5d94f80352515603a5b4bab9d2742b6ed80) and [IBQ checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/TencentARC/IBQ-Tokenizer-16384/tree/ef2200997f97118050c5b17dde48e8b78690f866). Downloaded from their publishers at pinned revisions.
